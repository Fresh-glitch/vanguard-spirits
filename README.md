# Vanguard Spirits

Some places were never abandoned. They were left in someone's care.

Far under the deepslate, past the last of the ore, the ground opens into rooms
that were cut rather than worn. Whoever built them meant to come back. Whoever
was left behind is still waiting.

## Echoes of the Past

Built for the CurseForge modjam of the same name.

The dark down there is not empty. It remembers, the way old stone does, and if
you stand still long enough you may catch it at it.

## Going down

Nothing in this mod will explain itself to you.

You will know a ruin when you find one, and you will know the moment you have
made a mistake inside it. Bring light. Bring more than you think you need.
Consider leaving before you are certain you have taken everything, because the
alternative is learning why the room needed watching.

If you carry something out, keep it on you. It is worth more than it looks, and
it behaves differently depending on what else you are carrying.

## Running it

Needs Fabric for Minecraft 26.2, Fabric API, and Fabric Language Kotlin.

To build from source:

    ./gradlew build

The jar lands in `build/libs`.
